How to read this

Scores run from 0 to 1. Each factor is the fraction that is the same between the lunar spot and the Earth site, the definition NASA uses to compare simulated lunar soil with real soil (Schrader et al. 2009). Weights and cut-offs are set by our team, not by NASA.

Data. Moon: NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LOLA elevation and slope, sunlight and permanent shadow maps, Diviner temperatures). Earth: Copernicus DEM 90 m (ESA), NASA MODIS vegetation and land surface temperature (2024), sun angles from latitude.

Limits. Earth library covers bare, snow-free land between 55 S and 84 N; Antarctica is not included. Region centres are approximate. No Earth site reproduces lunar gravity, vacuum or radiation.
