## How each candidate was picked

A fixed rule, applied the same way to every lunar target:

1. Search the whole world for the lunar square, with the purpose shown.
2. Walk down the ranked list (best square per 2 degree region).
3. Take the first site whose terrain score is 0.87 or higher ("same kind of terrain") and whose altitude is 3,500 m or lower. If no site reaches 0.87 under that altitude, take the first at 0.80 or higher ("possible match") and say so.

Altitude comes from the tool's display terrain tiles, not from the scoring data. Sites skipped for altitude are listed, because they are the better terrain matches.

## Limits

- Not covered: rock and soil type, dust, access, permits, land ownership, weather.
