## Removed after checking

- "Younger basalt caps" in the East Greenland geology line: the wording could not be confirmed in the text of GEUS Report 1998/28 that was read, so the claim was deleted.

## Qualified after checking

- The GEUS quote describes the area studied at about 73.5 to 74 N (Andree Land), not the whole 72 to 74 N belt. The brief now says so.
- UNESCO describes a cold continental desert environment on the high Pamir plateau, alongside glaciated mountains and lakes. The brief now limits the statement to the plateau.
